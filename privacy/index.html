Privacy Policy

METOSAN for iPhone, iPad and Mac · Effective November 13, 2026 · danbee Inc.

1. Overview

danbee Inc. (“we”) provides METOSAN, a character-based metronome application for iPhone, iPad and Mac. This policy describes how information is handled in the current version of the app on all supported platforms.

2. Information collected by the app

The current version of METOSAN does not require an account and does not collect or transmit personal information, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or location information to danbee Inc. through the app. No third-party advertising or analytics SDK is used in the current app version.

3. Information stored on your device

METOSAN stores preferences such as tempo, rhythm, sound, volume, language, theme colors, custom text, and your selected character locally on your device to remember your choices. These settings are not sent to our servers by the app.

4. In-app purchases

  • Optional purchases. METOSAN can be used free of charge with the default character. Additional characters are offered as optional one-time in-app purchases.
  • Payments are processed by Apple. All purchases are made and processed through Apple’s App Store using your Apple Account. danbee Inc. does not receive or store your payment card details, billing address, or Apple Account credentials. Apple handles this information under the Apple Privacy Policy.
  • Verifying your purchases. The app uses Apple’s StoreKit framework to check which characters you own and unlock them. The purchase status provided by Apple is used on your device and is not sent to our servers.
  • Universal purchase. METOSAN is a universal purchase app. Characters you buy are linked to your Apple Account and are available on your iPhone, iPad and Mac signed in with the same Apple Account, without paying again.
  • Restoring purchases. If a purchased character does not appear, for example after reinstalling the app or on a new device, use “Restore Purchases” in the Character Store. Restoring retrieves your purchase history from Apple.
  • Sales reports. Apple provides us with aggregated sales and financial reports through App Store Connect. These reports do not identify you personally.
  • Refunds. Refund requests are handled by Apple. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

5. Support inquiries

If you voluntarily contact us by email, we will receive the email address and information you include in your message. We use this information only to respond to and manage your inquiry. Such correspondence may be stored in our email system and is not part of automatic app data collection. Do not include sensitive personal information or payment details in support messages.

6. Website information

When you visit our website, its hosting and infrastructure providers may process technical data, such as IP addresses and request logs, for website delivery, reliability, and security. This is separate from the METOSAN app's local operation.

7. Sharing and security

We do not sell personal information. We may use service providers to operate the website and handle voluntary support correspondence, subject to applicable law and appropriate safeguards.

8. Changes to this policy

If future versions introduce account services, analytics, cloud synchronization, or other data processing, we will update this policy and relevant App Store privacy disclosures before or when those features become available. This policy does not authorize undisclosed future data collection.

9. Contact

danbee Inc. · contact@danbee.ai
