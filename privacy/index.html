Privacy Policy

METOSAN for macOS · Effective November 4, 2026 · danbee Inc.

1. Overview

danbee Inc. (“we”) provides METOSAN, a character-based metronome application for macOS. This policy describes how information is handled in the current version of the app.

2. Information collected by the app

The current version of METOSAN does not require an account and does not collect or transmit personal information, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or location information to danbee Inc. through the app. No third-party advertising or analytics SDK is used in the current app version.

3. Information stored on your device

METOSAN stores preferences such as tempo, rhythm, sound, language, and appearance locally on your device to remember your choices. These settings are not sent to our servers by the app.

4. Support inquiries

If you voluntarily contact us by email, we will receive the email address and information you include in your message. We use this information only to respond to and manage your inquiry. Such correspondence may be stored in our email system and is not part of automatic app data collection. Do not include sensitive personal information in support messages.

5. Website information

When you visit our website, its hosting and infrastructure providers may process technical data, such as IP addresses and request logs, for website delivery, reliability, and security. This is separate from the METOSAN app's local operation.

6. Sharing and security

We do not sell personal information. We may use service providers to operate the website and handle voluntary support correspondence, subject to applicable law and appropriate safeguards.

7. Changes to this policy

If future versions introduce account services, analytics, cloud synchronization, or other data processing, we will update this policy and relevant App Store privacy disclosures before or when those features become available. This policy does not authorize undisclosed future data collection.

8. Contact

danbee Inc. · contact@danbee.ai
